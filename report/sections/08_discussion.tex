\section{Discussion, limitations, and future directions}
\label{sec:discussion}

\subsection{What the cases establish (Q4, Q5)}
The study establishes a regime map rather than a universal ranking. FR is a mild
accelerator, and sometimes a source of extra noise, when ABF is already
well-sampled. It becomes valuable when ABF is starved of reaction-coordinate
support. In the many-body WCA case the same mechanism has a sharper operational
limit: too much birth--death destroys ancestor diversity faster than the dense
solvent can decorrelate cloned configurations. The different conclusions across
examples are therefore not a defect of the study; they are the main information
returned by the three-case design. The WCA phase diagram and its follow-up
analyses (\cref{sec:wca_phase_diagram,sec:wca_starvation}) make the decision rule
operational: the \emph{measured} ABF free-energy error predicts the mFR gain
($\rho=\WCAFStarvRhoErr$) far better than the nominal product $\beta h$
($\rho=\WCAFStarvRhoBetaH$), and the equal-compute control (\cref{sec:wca_equal_compute})
and frozen-bias reconstruction (\cref{sec:wca_frozen}) attribute the starved-regime
gain to the birth--death correction and to a genuinely more accurate learned bias,
not to extra raw compute or an online transient.

The molecular torsions (Q5) add a decisive qualification to this rule: the starvation
that FR can cure must be on the \emph{biased} coordinate. In butane ABF is not starved
along $\varphi_1$, so mFR is superfluous (\textbf{\AlkButaneVerdict} within the
pre-registered margin). In pentane the biased $\varphi_1$ marginal is again easy, but
the binding difficulty is the hidden conditional $p(\varphi_2\mid\varphi_1)$ --- and
because FR only reallocates along $\varphi_1$, it cannot directly relieve that
difficulty and risks a ``false improvement'' in which the marginal flattens while the
conditional degrades. The configuration-resolved conditional diagnostics, which the
2D and WCA cases could only infer from structure, make this measurable directly.

\subsection{Limitations}
We remain deliberately conservative about scope.
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Coordinate dimensionality and curvature.} The core cases use a
        one-dimensional reaction coordinate (a Cartesian component in the 2D toys, a
        scalar bond length in WCA, a signed dihedral in the alkanes). The
        collective-variable extension (\cref{sec:cv_harder_1d,sec:cv_2d}) lifts this for
        the alkanes: a strongly curved distance CV $R_{15}=|q_5-q_1|$ and a genuine
        two-dimensional torsion CV $(\varphi_1,\varphi_2)\in\mathbb{T}^2$ with the exact
        \emph{vector} generalized mean force (Gram matrix, dual fields, analytically
        differentiated divergence) and a periodic FFT Poisson projection. Higher-dimensional
        CVs and explicit path collective variables remain untested.
  \item \textbf{The oracle target is not a deployable method.} In every
        case the oracle uses the reference free energy, the unknown being
        computed, and is reported only as a diagnostic control (\cref{rem:oracle}).
        Its behaviour is read comparatively: best in the easy metastability
        regime, tied with practical targets in WCA, and worse than ABF in the cold
        base entropic bottleneck. In the dedicated entropy-dominant stress test it
        has a narrower interpretation: because \cref{sec:eb_obstruction} shows that
        trajectory-only target estimators collapse to the ABF bias or to the bias
        plus noise, the oracle curve is an information benchmark for what could be
        achieved only by adding external structure, not by tuning the same online
        estimator.
  \item \textbf{Production estimators are validated surrogates.} The 2D GPU
        backend uses a binned-smoothed mean-force/marginal estimator and a
        vectorised fixed-$N$ birth--death variant; the WCA backend uses a
        constrained projection with the geometric term and a direct
        death-replacement step. These pass their validation suites and agree with
        the kernel/TI references to within tolerance, but are not bit-identical to
        the original kernel notebook implementation.
  \item \textbf{Conditional diagnostics are incomplete in the many-body case.}
        The entropic-bottleneck case gives an analytic conditional check and shows
        no systematic collapse, but FR is not uniformly better than ABF in every
        bin. The WCA conditional fidelity is argued structurally
        (\cref{rem:manybody}) rather than measured per configuration.
  \item \textbf{Entropic-specific gains are real but target-limited.} In the base
        Case~II parameterisation the energetic double well dominates the difficulty
        and the $\omega_{\mathrm{in}}$ sweep is honestly flat. The dedicated
        entropy-dominant stress test (\cref{sec:eb_entropy_dominant}), which holds
        the \emph{total} barrier fixed and grows the entropic share, sharpens this:
        the \emph{deployable} gain still does not scale with the entropic fraction
        (slope $\EDBestSlope\%$ per unit $\phi$), whereas the \emph{oracle} gain
        does (slope $\EDBoracleSlope\%$, up to $+\EDBoraclePeak\%$). The
        entropic-specific benefit exists but is gated by the quality of the online
        free-energy target; the practical estimator does not realise it here.
  \item \textbf{The molecular negative rests on a single starved cell.} Across every
        coordinate we screened, exactly one is classified \emph{starved} along the biased
        CV --- pentane $R_{15}$ at $\beta=2$ (\cref{sec:cv_harder_1d}) --- with butane
        $R_{14}$ at $\beta=3$ only \emph{intermediate}. The claim that mFR fails to rescue
        a starved ABF is therefore established on one cell, hardened by matched seeds, an
        oracle target, an independent OPES sweep, a run-length control and a frozen-bias
        reconstruction, but not replicated on a second independent molecular system. It is
        also a statement about one mFR design (fixed-population kill-and-clone on the
        biased marginal at a rate frozen from a monotone four-point ladder): schemes that
        decorrelate clones after reallocation are untested and are not excluded by these
        data. \emph{Partly addressed.} The two all-atom dipeptides of
        \cref{sec:neutrality} are independent molecular systems and independently return a
        null --- but they are nulls of a different kind, ABF-sufficient rather than starved,
        so they replicate the \emph{neutrality} result and not the starvation result. The
        discovery-limited claim still rests on $R_{15}$ alone.
  \item \textbf{The positive regime is now demonstrated on a system built for it.}
        \Cref{sec:gateway} produces the establishment-limited regime deliberately rather
        than hoping to find it, and supplies the sham control the earlier positives lack.
        That is a strength for attribution and a limitation for generality: the anchor is a
        two-dimensional model with an analytic free energy, and the only \emph{many-body}
        establishment-limited evidence remains the WCA dimer, where no sham arm was run. The
        natural next step is to port the matched sham to the WCA cells, which requires no new
        physics and would test whether the attribution transfers.
  \item \textbf{One artifact behind a reported number no longer exists.} The alanine
        $N=2048$ stage's decision record was overwritten by a later stage that wrote
        unsuffixed filenames, so the numbers quoted in \cref{sec:ala} are from the $N=4096$
        stage, whose artifact survives. The raw trajectories are retained and the overwritten
        stage reproduces on recomputation, but a result whose artifact is gone should be
        labelled rather than quietly re-derived.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Failure modes}
The analysis points to two related failure modes. First, an \emph{overly
aggressive mechanism} erodes ancestor diversity faster than the orthogonal
coordinates can decorrelate clones. This is realised in the WCA dimer for
\texttt{fr\_rate}$\ge1$ in the rate ladder, with even \texttt{fr\_rate}$=0.5$
turning harmful at production budget, and is masked in the entropic bottleneck
because its analytic channel re-equilibrates rapidly. Second, a \emph{misaligned
target} can hurt when it over-commits to a fixed shape during a transient ABF
bias. The metastability oracle--estimated gap quantifies target-direction
headroom only in the easy regime; the entropic-bottleneck oracle shows that this
reading does not generalise.

\subsection{Future directions}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Adaptive, diversity-aware FR strength.} We implement and test a
        first version of this (\cref{sec:wca_adaptive}, method
        \texttt{fr\_estimated\_adaptive}): the effective rate is a product of an
        online marginal-mismatch support gate, an ancestor-ESS diversity gate that
        halts before collapse, and an event-fraction backoff. Extending the gates
        to orthogonal-relaxation diagnostics and to multi-dimensional reaction
        coordinates remains open.
  \item \textbf{Higher-dimensional and curved reaction coordinates.} The alkane
        dihedrals (\cref{sec:case_butane,sec:case_pentane}) take a first step ---
        a periodic, nonlinear CV with a nontrivial geometric term --- and the
        collective-variable extension completes it: a curved distance CV
        (\cref{sec:cv_harder_1d}) and a genuine 2-D torus CV $(\varphi_1,\varphi_2)$
        with the exact vector mean force and periodic Poisson reconstruction
        (\cref{sec:cv_2d}). What remains open is \emph{higher} dimension (three or more
        CVs, where the density estimate degrades faster than the sampling improves) and
        path collective variables, where the CV itself is learned rather than chosen.
  \item \textbf{Breaking the clone-correlation bottleneck.} The decisive $R_{15}$
        finding --- that reallocation repairs nominal support while destroying lineage
        diversity (\cref{sec:cv_does_it_help}) --- identifies the mechanism a useful
        birth--death scheme would have to fix: clones must decorrelate before the
        mean-force estimator consumes them. Rejuvenation moves after cloning (a short
        randomised or tempered relaxation of the orthogonal coordinates), or a rate that
        adapts to the measured orthogonal relaxation time rather than to the marginal
        mismatch alone, are the natural candidates; neither is tested here.
  \item \textbf{Configuration-resolved conditional diagnostics.} Pentane realises
        this directly: $p(\varphi_2\mid\varphi_1)$ against the joint reference
        measures conditional fidelity rather than inferring it from structure,
        exactly separating genuine from false improvement.
  \item \textbf{Adding information for entropic bottlenecks.} The
        entropy-dominant stress test (\cref{sec:eb_entropy_dominant}) shows the
        achievable (oracle) gain is entropic-specific, but
        \cref{sec:eb_obstruction} shows a lower-variance online estimator on the
        same trajectory \emph{cannot} realise it: moment fusion, a transverse
        control variate, and confidence gating all reduce to the ABF bias or to
        the bias plus noise, so the oracle is an information upper bound rather
        than a tuning target. The open route is therefore to change the available
        information --- enhanced sampling of a genuinely slow orthogonal
        coordinate, multiple-replica or tempered data, or a parametric prior on
        the channel stiffness $\omega(x)$ fit from the well interiors --- not a
        cleverer estimator on the same data.
\end{itemize}
