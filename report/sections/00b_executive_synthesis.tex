\section{Executive synthesis}
\label{sec:executive}

This section states the v1 result in full before any of the machinery. Everything asserted
here is traceable to \cref{tab:closure_inventory,tab:regime_map} and, through them, to the
artifact-derived inventory\\
\texttt{results/closure/v1\_results\_inventory.csv}.

\paragraph{What marginal Fisher--Rao changes.}
ABF learns a bias from a mean force estimated on whatever configurations its walkers happen
to occupy. The marginal Fisher--Rao (mFR) step adds one thing to that: at fixed population
size it kills replicas where the empirical reaction-coordinate marginal $\phat_t$ exceeds a
target $q_t$ and clones replicas where it falls short. It moves \emph{existing} walkers
between regions of the biased coordinate. It does not change the potential, the estimator, or
the dynamics between events, and it cannot put a walker anywhere that no walker has already
reached.

\paragraph{Four finite-time regimes.}
Which of those properties binds is decided by three timescales measured against the run length:
how long a relevant state takes to be \textbf{found}, how long its orthogonal degrees of
freedom take to \textbf{equilibrate} at fixed reaction coordinate, and how long it then takes
to be adequately \textbf{populated}. Every benchmark in this report falls into one of the
resulting regimes (\cref{tab:regime_map}), classified by the \emph{first} gate it fails.

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Conditional-equilibration-limited} --- the orthogonal ensemble has not
  equilibrated, so the ABF mean force is an average over a non-equilibrium conditional and the
  baseline itself is invalid. Deca-alanine alone; the $\beta=2$ cells of $R_{15}$ fail the
  same statistic only over a local band and remain discovery-limited (\cref{sec:r15_audits}).
  By
  \cref{prop:sel_invariance} no marginal selection rule can help; more importantly, no
  discovery or establishment verdict computed on such a baseline is admissible. Deca-alanine
  passes both of those gates and would have been filed as a target for the method, which is
  why this check now runs first.
  \item \textbf{ABF-sufficient} --- found early, populated early. Butane and pentane on
  $\varphi_1$, pentane on the torsion torus $(\varphi_1,\varphi_2)$, both dipeptides, the
  metastability model, the entropic bottleneck at $\beta\le4$, and both solvated
  literature benchmarks --- methane association (\cref{sec:methane}) and sodium chloride
  association (\cref{sec:nacl}), the latter carrying a real $\NaBarrierKT\,k_BT$ desolvation
  barrier and still showing no deficit. There is no marginal
  imbalance left for reallocation to correct.
  \item \textbf{Discovery-limited} --- not found. Pentane's end-to-end distance $R_{15}$ at
  $\beta=2$, the only genuinely starved biased coordinate in the study. Selection amplifies a
  configuration that exists; it cannot manufacture a torsional crossing.
  \item \textbf{Establishment-limited} --- found early, populated slowly. The constructed
  entropic gateway, the condensed-phase WCA dimer, and the entropic bottleneck at $\beta=8$.
  This is the regime mFR serves.
\end{itemize}

Two consequences follow that a reader should have before the case sections. First, the regime
mFR serves has never yet been found in a system we did not build: the gateway was constructed to
contain it, and the two solvated literature benchmarks entered to look for it in nature both
came back ABF-sufficient. Since NaCl was the designated venue for the comparison against prior
directed selection, that comparison stands unanswered outside the WCA dimer, where it ended in a
tie. Second, and independently of any one system, a cross-system audit over $\VmRuns$ runs finds
that \emph{no deployable ABF-only indicator predicts the sign of the mFR gain out of sample}
(\cref{sec:mechanism_audit}); the preregistered gate scores $\VmGateAuc$, below chance, with a
sign that flips between held-out systems. Deciding when to switch the mechanism on therefore
requires the regime diagnosis of this report, which requires a reference free energy --- the
quantity the method is there to compute.

\paragraph{The two positives, and the control that earns them.}
Both establishment-limited systems were tested against a \emph{matched-turnover sham}: the
same number of kill-and-clone events, at the same times, with the direction randomised. This
is the control that separates Fisher--Rao steering from resampling of the same intensity, and
until it existed ``mFR helps'' and ``any turnover of this magnitude helps'' were
indistinguishable.

On the constructed gateway, at a cell classified and frozen before any Fisher--Rao arm was
written, practical mFR improves the time-integrated free-energy error by
$\ClosureGwTwoGain\%$ on $\ClosureGwTwoWins$ fresh seeds in the quoted replicate
($\ClosureGwOneGain\%$ on $\ClosureGwOneWins$ in the first pass), and beats its own sham
directly in both. On the WCA dimer, with no hyperparameter reopened and $16$ fresh seeds, it
improves the same endpoint by $\WcvEstGain\%$ on $\WcvEstWins$ seeds and beats its own
sham by $\WcvDirect\%$ on $\WcvDirectWins$. Neither sham is resolvably better than ABF: the
gateway's lands on the adverse side of zero and WCA's spans it
($\WcvShamGain\%$ on $\WcvShamWins$ seeds). (The WCA figures are scored against a corrected
high-precision reference; the cached one used first was wrong inside the transition region
and had put this headline at $\ClosureWcaGain\%$.)

That gain is attributable to \emph{directed} selection, but not to \emph{this} direction. At
matched turnover against the two directed rules already in use, mFR is tied by simple count
balancing ($\QoneCountGain\%$, TOST interval $[\QoneCountTostLo, \QoneCountTostHi]$ inside
$\pm\QoneTostMargin\%$), which reaches $\QoneCountVsAbf\%$ against ABF on its own, while the
Chapter~6 Laplacian rule is inert ($\QoneBookVsAbf\%$). The claim this report can support is a
principled rule consistent with the establishment mechanism, not a performance win over prior
art.

Barrier round trips in the WCA test differ by $\WcvRoundTripPct\%$ between mFR and ABF
while the free-energy error differs by $\WcvEstGain\%$ --- roughly a
\WcvRoundTripSeparation-fold separation between the transport change and the accuracy change,
both measured on the same runs against the same reference. In this setup the mechanism
is therefore consistent with redistributing population among replicas that were already
crossing, rather than with buying additional crossings.

\paragraph{The boundary cases, which are not failed benchmarks.}
Four molecular systems and two model potentials return neutral or adverse results, and they
are load-bearing rather than embarrassing: they are what makes the positive claim narrow
enough to be useful. Butane, pentane on $\varphi_1$, the torsion torus, and both dipeptides
establish that when ABF already resolves the slow coordinate along the biased CV, directed
reallocation has no work left to do --- in the alanine dipeptide even the \emph{oracle}
target, built from the reference free energy itself, changes nothing across a $22\times$
range of realised event rate. Pentane's $R_{15}$ establishes the harder point: making the
biased coordinate genuinely starved is \emph{not} sufficient, because that starvation is a
dynamical bottleneck rather than a support deficit, and pushing mFR hard enough to repair the
nominal support makes the free energy substantially worse while ancestor diversity collapses
and round trips fall. Amplification is not discovery.

\paragraph{Principal limitations.}
The positive rests on two systems, one of them constructed for the purpose. The gateway's
sham \emph{equivalence} test passes in the first confirmatory pass and misses the
preregistered margin in the replicate, so equivalence is not established there and the claim
rests on the direct arm-versus-sham contrast instead; on the WCA dimer that direct contrast
was preregistered, and the sham fails equivalence in the adverse direction. No practical
(estimated-target) mFR arm was ever run on alanine, and no mFR arm at all was run on valine,
whose screen stopped the study before one was justified. The deployable estimated target
remains the binding constraint wherever an oracle target shows headroom. And every conclusion
is conditioned on the specific model, coordinate, budget, and implementation tested here.

Finally, a parallel audit line in this repository --- forked from the same commit, closed
separately, and \emph{not} merged --- reaches more conservative conclusions on the WCA system:
it finds the cached thermodynamic-integration reference inflates the measured improvement by
about $2\times$ on a related endpoint, it rejects variance reduction as the mechanism, and it
reports the Fisher--Rao score as no better than plain count balancing. All three have since
been checked directly and all three hold: the reference was rebuilt and corrected, the
variance-reduction language has been withdrawn in favour of coverage equalisation, and the
count-balancing claim now has a preregistered turnover-matched test behind it. What remains
unreconciled is only that the two lines use different endpoints, so their effect sizes are not
directly comparable; \cref{rem:audit_line} states the current position.
