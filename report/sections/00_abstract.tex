We study whether augmenting the Adaptive Biasing Force (ABF) method with a
Fisher--Rao (FR) birth--death correction improves the computation of a
reaction-coordinate free energy, and what kind of improvement it can reasonably be
expected to provide. We test this on five core case studies (then extended along the
collective variable itself) spanning model potentials,
a condensed-phase dimer, and molecular torsions: a two-dimensional double-well
\emph{metastability} model with a quadrature reference; a two-dimensional
\emph{entropic-bottleneck} model with a narrow transverse channel, for which both
the reference free energy and the conditional law
$Y\mid X=x\sim\mathcal N(0,1/(\beta\omega(x)^2))$ are analytic; a many-body
condensed-phase \emph{Weeks--Chandler--Andersen (WCA) dimer} in a dense solvent,
evaluated against a high-accuracy thermodynamic-integration reference; and two
united-atom alkanes whose slow coordinate is a signed dihedral --- \emph{butane}
(one torsion, an easy control with an exact analytic reference) and \emph{pentane}
(two torsions coupled by the 1--5 ``pentane effect'', so that the second dihedral is
a hidden slow coordinate when only the first is biased). The alkanes use the correct
nonlinear generalized mean force (including the geometric/curvature term) and
periodic estimators. In each case the FR step reallocates walkers along the reaction
coordinate by killing replicas in over-represented regions and cloning whole
configurations from under-represented regions, nudging the empirical marginal
$\phat_t$ toward a target $q_t$.

The central finding is not a single universal success story, but a regime map. In
the metastability model, where plain ABF already samples reasonably well,
estimated-target FR is a modest accelerator and the oracle diagnostic shows that
target direction can matter. In the cold entropic bottleneck, ABF is starved and
estimated-target FR gives a large gain ($+\EBgainStrong\%$, $\EBcoldWin/20$ seeds),
but the uniform target nearly matches it and the oracle target is worse than ABF;
here density correction and free-energy accuracy are not the same objective. A
dedicated entropy-dominant stress test (transverse dimension $m$, total barrier
held fixed) refines this further: as the barrier is made more entropic the
\emph{achievable} (oracle) gain grows specifically with the entropic share, yet the
\emph{deployable} estimated target does not capture it --- an entropic-specific
benefit that is real but target-limited. In
the WCA dimer, tuned FR again improves the free energy reliably
($+\WCAtunedGainPct\%$, $\WCAtunedWin/10$ seeds), while the best accuracy occurs
near the edge of an accuracy--diversity tradeoff. The molecular cases sharpen the
picture: in \emph{butane}, ABF already resolves the only slow coordinate, so mFR
turns itself off (its birth--death rate falls to $\approx\AlkButaneFrEvent$ and its
free-energy error matches ABF, \textbf{\AlkButaneVerdict} within a pre-registered
$\pm10\%$ practical-equivalence margin) --- the extra mechanism has no return where
it is not needed, rather than failing. In \emph{pentane}, where a second coupled
dihedral makes the conditional law $p(\varphi_2|\varphi_1)$ the binding difficulty,
we separate genuine improvement (better $F(\varphi_1)$ with faithful conditional)
from false improvement (flatter marginal but corrupted conditional), and report the
matched-seed outcome the data support rather than assuming the coupled case must be
positive. A final study varies the \emph{collective variable itself} to stress-test the
mechanism: replacing the easy torsion with a harder chemically meaningful scalar --- the
pentane end-to-end distance $R_{15}$ --- makes ABF genuinely sample-starved \emph{along the
biased coordinate} at low temperature (the first starved coordinate in the study), yet even
here mFR gives no improvement (equivalent at the deployable rate, monotonically worse and
lineage-collapsing as it is pushed), because that starvation is a \emph{dynamical} bottleneck
--- rare torsional-barrier crossings --- that marginal reallocation can only amplify, not
create; and promoting the hidden torsion into a genuine two-dimensional CV $(\varphi_1,
\varphi_2)$, with an exact vector generalized mean force and a periodic FFT Poisson
projection, removes the starvation entirely (ABF resolves both barriers directly), so mFR is
again neutral, now for a well-understood reason. Across all cases, FR is best understood as a
marginal-reallocation mechanism whose value depends on how starved the ABF estimator is
\emph{along the biased coordinate}, whether that starvation is a support deficit reallocation
can fill or a dynamical bottleneck it cannot, how quickly cloned orthogonal degrees of freedom
re-equilibrate, and how aggressively birth--death erodes ancestor diversity. The oracle target is
therefore a diagnostic control, not a deployable method and not a universal upper
bound.
