\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Across five core controlled systems --- a 2D metastability model, a 2D entropic
bottleneck with an analytic conditional law, a many-body condensed-phase WCA dimer
with a high-accuracy TI reference, and two united-atom alkane torsions --- together with a
collective-variable extension (a harder curved distance CV and a genuine two-dimensional
torsion CV), Fisher--Rao birth--death helps ABF only after one specifies the regime. In the easy metastability
setting it is a modest accelerator and the oracle diagnostic suggests target direction
still matters. In the cold entropic bottleneck it gives a large practical gain
($+\EBgainStrong\%$, $\EBcoldWin/20$ seeds), but the oracle target is worse than
ABF, showing that perfect reference-shaped density steering is not the same as
best ABF accuracy. In the WCA dimer tuned FR reliably improves the free energy
($+\WCAtunedGainPct\%$, $\WCAtunedWin/10$ seeds), while stronger FR exposes the
accuracy--diversity boundary.

The molecular torsions add the sharpest statement. Butane's single dihedral is
resolved by ABF alone, so marginal FR turns itself off and is
\textbf{\AlkButaneVerdict} to ABF within a pre-registered $\pm10\%$ margin: a case
where the honest conclusion is ``no meaningful gain because none is needed'', not
``the method fails''. Pentane exposes the mechanism's blind spot: FR reallocates mass
along the biased dihedral $\varphi_1$, which is easy to flatten, whereas the true
difficulty is the hidden conditional $p(\varphi_2\mid\varphi_1)$ set by the 1--5
coupling --- a coordinate FR never biases. We therefore report, from matched seeds,
whether cloning whole configurations helps or corrupts that conditional, distinguishing
genuine from false improvement rather than reading marginal flatness as success.

Varying the collective variable itself sharpens this into a necessary condition.
Replacing the easy torsion with a harder curved distance CV ($R_{15}$) does make ABF
genuinely sample-starved along the biased coordinate at low temperature --- the first such
case in the study --- yet mFR still gives no gain: it is equivalent at the deployable rate
and monotonically worse (with collapsing lineage) as it is pushed, because that starvation is
a \emph{dynamical} bottleneck (rare torsional-barrier crossings) that reallocation can only
amplify, not create. Promoting the hidden torsion into a genuine two-dimensional CV
$(\varphi_1,\varphi_2)$ instead \emph{removes} the starvation --- biasing both dihedrals
directly resolves both barriers --- so mFR is neutral again, now for a fully understood
reason. The lesson is that starvation of the biased coordinate is necessary but not
sufficient: mFR helps only when that starvation is a \emph{support deficit} its reallocation
can fill, not a dynamical barrier it cannot.

Two all-atom dipeptides then close the molecular question, and a constructed model answers
the one it leaves open. Vacuum alanine and valine (\cref{sec:neutrality}) were both chosen as
systems where mFR ought to help, both cleared every gate preceding the decisive one, and both
returned \textbf{\AlaVerdict}. Valine is the more informative of the two because the reason it
was selected proved measurable and wrong: its $11$--$18\,k_BT$ side-chain barrier is a
backbone-\emph{clamped conditional} barrier, worth $1.1$--$7.4\,k_BT$ with the backbone free,
and the genuinely slow coordinate is the backbone $\varphi$ --- which is in the CV. Both
peptides therefore fail the same necessary condition, and for the same reason as the alkanes:
ABF discovers and populates every state by itself.

Read together, the systems sort into three regimes rather than along one axis
(\cref{sec:three_regimes}): \emph{ABF-sufficient} (found early, populated early --- the
alkanes, the two-dimensional torus CV, both dipeptides; mFR neutral), \emph{discovery-limited}
(never found --- $R_{15}$; mFR cannot act), and \emph{establishment-limited} (found early,
populated slowly), which is the only regime mFR serves and the only one that never arose on
its own. Continuing to sample molecules would not have fixed that, because the regime is
decided by whether the slow coordinate is inside the collective variable --- a property of the
coordinate, not of the method. We therefore construct the regime instead
(\cref{sec:gateway}), in an entropic-gateway model whose free energy is analytic, screen it
with ABF alone across a $\GwCells$-cell finite-budget map, and freeze both the classification
and the anchor cell before running any Fisher--Rao arm. A calibration pass fixes the rates; a
separate \emph{confirmatory} run on $\GwcSeeds$ fresh seeds, with no ladder and no tuning,
then meets every criterion of a success rule written down in advance: $\GwcEstGain\%$
$[\GwcEstCIlo, \GwcEstCIhi]$ on $\GwcEstWins$ seeds with both diversity gates passing. The
effect survives the control in which discovery is handed to the sampler for free, and it
survives an estimator-independent endpoint in which each arm's learned bias is frozen and
scored by a fresh population with no adaptation and no birth--death ($\GwcFrozen\%$).

The control that matters most is the one this study previously lacked entirely. Every
Fisher--Rao arm anywhere in this work derives its replacement direction from the same score
and differs only in how the target is built, so ``the gain comes from balanced resampling''
had never been tested. A \textbf{matched sham} --- the same number of clone and delete events
at the same times, with which walkers they act on randomised --- never improves on ABF in
either pass ($\GwcShamGain\%$ and $\GwrShamGain\%$), and mFR beats it \emph{directly}, on
the same seeds and the same event schedule, by $\GwcDirect\%$
$[\GwcDirectCIlo, \GwcDirectCIhi]$ and $\GwrDirect\%$ $[\GwrDirectCIlo, \GwrDirectCIhi]$.

Two honest qualifications travel with that. The formal equivalence test on the sham passes in
the first pass and misses in the second by about four tenths of a percentage point
(\cref{rem:gw_replicate}); since both point estimates put the sham on the \emph{harmful} side
of ABF, that failure cannot mean turnover explains the gain, but the preregistered rule was
written without distinguishing the direction of such a failure and we report it as written.
And on the frozen-bias endpoint the first pass shows uniform turnover contributing a small
share of the benefit, so the split is quantified rather than presented as a clean dichotomy.

The attribution then transfers to the many-body system. At the accepted WCA-dimer cell, on
\WcsSeeds{} fresh seeds with nothing retuned, practical mFR improves the integrated error by
\WcsEstGain\% $[\WcsEstCIlo, \WcsEstCIhi]$ on \WcsEstWins{} seeds and beats its own matched
sham by \WcsDirect\% $[\WcsDirectCIlo, \WcsDirectCIhi]$, while matched random turnover is
itself \emph{not} an improvement on ABF ($\WcsShamGain\%$). Round trips differ by under
$1\%$ across arms, so the mechanism redistributes population rather than creating crossings ---
the behaviour the two-state reduction predicts, confirmed on a system it was not built from.

One limit is worth stating in the same breath. The $\beta$ axis of the gateway map is a
\emph{time-budget} axis, not a family of landscapes: $\beta F$ is identical in every cell by
construction, and under $\tau = t/\beta$ the longitudinal dynamics is exactly $\beta$-free
(\cref{rem:gw_beta}). The map says which budgets put a system in which regime, not which
potentials do.

The common object is marginal reallocation, not a universal variance-reduction
slogan. FR moves whole configurations from over-represented to under-represented
reaction-coordinate regions. When ABF is starved by a support deficit, this improves weighted
support for the mean-force estimator; when ABF is already well sampled, or starved by a
dynamical bottleneck, it can add noise;
and when the rate is too high, it collapses ancestor diversity. The oracle target
is useful precisely because it reveals these regimes: it is best in the easy case,
irrelevant in WCA, and harmful in the cold bottleneck. The practical recommendation
is therefore regime-aware: use the estimated target, choose a moderate rate
calibrated to orthogonal relaxation and ancestor diversity, and expect the largest
returns where ABF alone lacks reaction-coordinate support.

Stated as a falsifiable claim: \emph{marginal Fisher--Rao reallocation does not accelerate
barrier discovery, and does not improve a free energy ABF already resolves. Its useful regime
is narrower and more specific --- a relevant state must already have been discovered, remain
distinguishable in the selected marginal, and be receiving population too slowly under
ordinary ABF.} Each of the three conditions is independently falsified somewhere in this
study, and all three hold together only where the method is shown to work.
