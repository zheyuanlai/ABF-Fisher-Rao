\section{A literature-anchored negative: methane association in water}
\label{sec:methane}

The gateway of \cref{sec:gateway} was constructed to contain the regime marginal reallocation
is supposed to serve. That construction is its strength as a mechanism test and its weakness as
evidence: a regime built to order says little about how often it occurs. The obvious complement
is a molecular system chosen for the opposite reason --- one where the literature already
describes the physics, where nothing about the setup was arranged by us, and where the honest
expectation is that mFR has no room to act.

Methane--methane association in explicit water is that system. Its potential of mean force is a
textbook case of hydrophobic association, with a contact pair, a desolvation barrier and a
solvent-separated pair; the reaction coordinate is a single interparticle distance, so nothing
is hidden by a poor choice of collective variable; and the orthogonal degrees of freedom are
real water molecules that must rearrange for the pair to separate. It was entered in the
preregistration as a \emph{likely-null falsification benchmark} before any methane code existed,
with the stop condition written in advance: if ABF is sufficient, report that as the result and
do not extend the budget until it stops being true.

\subsection{Model, engine and reference}

The physical model follows Asthagiri, Merchant and Pratt: united-atom methane
($\sigma_{\mathrm M} = \MeSigmaM\,\text{\AA}$, $\epsilon_{\mathrm M} = \MeEpsM$~kcal/mol) in
$\MeNwater$ rigid SPC/E waters at $\MeTemp$~K, particle-mesh Ewald, a
$\MeCut$~nm cutoff, and a $\MeDt$~fs BAOAB timestep. The methane--oxygen unlike-pair rule is
Lorentz--Berthelot and is declared as our implementation choice rather than as a value read from
that paper. NPT equilibration fixes the production box at $L = \MeBoxL \pm \MeBoxLerr$~nm, giving
a density of $\MeDensity$~g/cm$^3$ against SPC/E's accepted $\sim 0.998$; all production is NVT
at that volume, so the target measure is exactly the canonical measure of \cref{sec:background}.

The sampler is a batched periodic engine written for this study, since no existing code runs a
population of walkers sharing one live ABF estimator. It agrees with OpenMM to
$\MeParityE$ in energy and $\MeParityF$ in force over configurations spanning
$-2598$ to $+45\,223$~kJ/mol, its constrained BAOAB reproduces OpenMM's thermostat to
$\MeThermoDelta$~K, and rigid-water constraints hold to $\MeConstraint$~nm.

The reference is constrained thermodynamic integration --- the same local mean force
$f = \tfrac12(\mathcal F_1 - \mathcal F_2)\cdot e - 2/(\beta r)$ that ABF itself estimates ---
over $\MeRefBuilds$ independent builds, each $r$ sampled from deliberately opposed \emph{wet} and
\emph{dry} solvent preparations. Builds agree to $\MeRefL2$~kJ/mol against a $\MeRefSpan$~kT span,
a ratio of $\MeRefRatio$ against an acceptance threshold of $0.5$.

\subsection{The landscape is real, and it is sub-$k_BT$}

Our reference reproduces the literature structure at the expected positions: a contact minimum at
$\MeContactR$~nm, a desolvation barrier at $\MeBarrierR$~nm and a solvent-separated minimum at
$\MeSSMR$~nm. The depths are the finding. Measured on $W(r)$ they are $\MeContactW$,
$\MeBarrierW$ and $\MeSSMW$~$k_BT$, so the barrier from the higher of the two minima is
$\MeBarrierHeight$~$k_BT$ --- below the $2\,k_BT$ merge threshold, which collapses the landscape
to a \emph{single basin} and fires the preregistered tercile fallback for state definitions.

This was recorded before the screen ran. There is no metastability in $\xi$ for ABF to be slow
about, and the prediction that follows --- that methane will be ABF-sufficient --- was written
down at the reference stage precisely so that it could not afterwards be presented as a
confirmed hypothesis.

\subsection{Gate 0: the baseline is sound}

Before asking what limits ABF, ask whether its conditional mean force can be trusted at all. The
instrument is the controlled experiment of \cref{sec:synthesis}: independently prepared solvent
families held at the same $r$, either agreeing or not. Methane's cross-family spread is
$\MeGateZeroGlobal$ of $\overline{|F'|}$ globally and $\MeGateZeroCore$ through the desolvation
region, against WCA's $\MeGateZeroWCA$ and deca-alanine's failing $\MeGateZeroDeca$. The spread
also \emph{collapsed} roughly threefold between the 50~ps and 200~ps checkpoints, which is the
signature of a conditional ensemble that mixes; deca's error persisted at up to $2\times10^{6}$
effective counts per bin.

Methane is therefore not conditional-equilibration-limited. Whatever the screen finds is a
statement about the free-energy landscape rather than about a broken baseline.

\subsection{The screen, and what each gate can and cannot support}

Eight independent ensembles of $\MeNwalk$ walkers ran $\MeRunPs$~ps of ABF with no Fisher--Rao
arm of any kind. All four gates pass and the classification is \emph{ABF-sufficient} on
$\MeSeeds$ of $\MeSeeds$ seeds, unanimously.

The result rests on Gate C, and the reasons the other gates carry less weight are worth stating
rather than hiding. Occupancy of every tercile tracks the bias-aware target
$Q^*_k(t) \propto \int \exp[-\beta(F_{\mathrm{ref}} - B_t)]$ throughout the second half of every
run: the worst ratio over $8$ seeds and $3$ states is $\MeOccRatioWorst$ and the best is
$\MeOccRatioBest$, against a deficit threshold of $0.5$. No partition choice can manufacture
that. Walkers never pin, with a maximum single-tercile occupancy of $\MePinned$ against a $0.90$
clause and against deca's $0.951$--$0.9996$.

Because the verdict rests on this gate, it is worth asking what deficit the gate could have
detected. Gate C compares an \emph{integer walker count} against $0.5\,Q^*_k$, so its power is
set by the expected count $\lambda_k = Q^*_k N$ and not by the run length or the number of
checkpoints. Once $0.5\lambda < 1$ the only integer satisfying the test is zero, and the gate
silently stops measuring a shortfall and starts measuring whether the state happens to be empty
at that instant --- an event with frequency $e^{-\lambda}$ on the physics alone, rising as $N$
falls. We therefore require $\lambda_k \geq \MeGateCLamMin$, the value at which a $50\,\%$
deficit is a two-sigma effect on a single checkpoint, and report any state below it as
non-binding with its detectable effect size rather than reading its output. Methane's three
terciles hold $\lambda = \MeGateCLamOne$, $\MeGateCLamTwo$ and $\MeGateCLamThree$ at the worst
checkpoint of the judged window, resolving $\MeGateCMdeLo$--$\MeGateCMdeHi\,\%$ deficits against
the $50\,\%$ the gate tests for, so the requirement is inert here by measurement rather than by
assumption. It is not inert in general: in the companion NaCl study the contact-pair state holds
$\lambda = 1.72$ at $N = 64$, where the smallest resolvable deficit is $152\,\%$, and at $N = 16$
and $N = 8$ no state clears the bar at all. Since the preregistered rule selects the
\emph{smallest} $N$ passing every gate, the search runs directly toward the cells in which the
gate cannot fail --- and this project has already retracted one screen in which Gate C fired on a
state that could never hold a walker and reported that a Fisher--Rao arm was licensed.

Gate B, by contrast, is \emph{vacuous here, and that is a property of the physics}. First
arrivals at thresholds well beyond the tercile boundary run only $\MeFarRatioLo$--$\MeFarRatioHi
\times$ the exact ballistic floor for the fastest of $\MeNwalk$ walkers, and the whole
evaluation domain is $\MeDomainWidth$~nm --- about $\MeBallisticSteps$ ballistic steps for the
leading walker. There is no separation between crossing a barrier and flying across the box, so
no discovery \emph{rate} is resolvable at any budget. The companion NaCl study runs the identical
gate on a $1.20$~nm domain and measures $\MeNaClFarLo$--$\MeNaClFarHi\times$ its floor: a
resolvable, unambiguously diffusive rate. The same gate, run the same way, is informative in one
system and vacuous in the other, and the discriminator is domain width in ballistic steps rather
than anything about the method.

Gate A passes at $\MeGateAPre$ against a $0.30$ threshold on the preregistered quantity,
$\mathrm{TV}(p(\xi \mid Y=a), p(\xi \mid Y=b))$ with $Y$ the inter-methane water count. The
collective variable does see the solvent physics. We note for the record that our first
implementation computed the transpose, $\mathrm{TV}(p(Y \mid \xi))$, which reads $
\MeGateATransposed$; that is a weaker and partly tautological statement, since the gap volume
grows with $r$ by construction, and it is not the number that licenses a marginal method to act.

A high total variation could still be near-tautological if the descriptor were simply a function
of the coordinate, so we measured that rather than assuming it. Across $0.01$~nm bins, $n_{\rm
gap}$ varies $\MeOrthoRatio\times$ more between bins than within them, retaining a standard
deviation of $\MeNgapSdFixedR$ at fixed $r$. The descriptor is substantially but not wholly
determined by the coordinate, so Gate A here is a genuine measurement. The companion NaCl study
measures $14$--$83\times$ on its hydration descriptors and a total variation of exactly $1.000$,
which its own analysis correctly reports as a statement about the physics --- ion hydration is
nearly a function of the ion separation --- rather than as a strong gate. Methane retains real
solvent structure orthogonal to $r$, which is what makes its null informative: there was
something for reallocation to act on, and it was still unnecessary.

\subsection{Finite size: a check that cannot resolve what it tests}

The preregistration required a finite-size check --- the constrained-TI mean force at
$r = 0.70, 0.80, 0.90$~nm recomputed in a $1024$-water box, with the evaluation domain to be
truncated if any point moved by more than $\MeFsTol\,k_BT/\text{nm}$. It was specified to run
\emph{before} the reference was built and was in fact run after it, so it can no longer gate the
domain; it can only report whether the outer points of an accepted reference are trustworthy.
Retrospectively performing a preregistered check is a weaker thing than fulfilling it, and we say
which one happened.

In the larger box ($L = \MeFsLbig$~nm, $\rho = \MeFsRhoBig$~g/cm$^3$, where $r = 0.90$ sits at
$\MeFsHalfBox$\% of the minimum-image half-box rather than $72$\%) the differences are
$+\MeFsDiffSeven$, $+\MeFsDiffEight$ and $+\MeFsDiffNine\,k_BT/\text{nm}$. Read literally the
gate fails at the outer two points. Read with the uncertainties --- $\pm\MeFsSemSeven$,
$\pm\MeFsSemEight$, $\pm\MeFsSemNine$ --- \emph{nothing is resolved}: the nominal failure at
$0.80$ is $0.21\sigma$ and the one at $0.90$ is $\MeFsSigNine\sigma$.

The tolerance is five to seven times finer than the uncertainty of the measurement it gates, and
closing that gap needs two orders of magnitude more sampling than the check itself cost. The
criterion was fixed in advance without asking whether it was measurable --- a check whose outcome
cannot entail the thing it is relied on to establish. We record the gate as \textbf{inconclusive}
rather than failed, treat $r = 0.90$ as suspect, and confirm the interior: at $r = 0.70$ the two
box sizes agree to $\MeFsDiffSeven\,k_BT/\text{nm}$, and every state boundary and essentially all
of the sampled population lie below that separation.

Whether the verdict depends on this was tested rather than argued. Perturbing the reference by
the \emph{full} measured difference at $0.80$ and $0.90$, re-integrating, and re-running Gate C
against the perturbed target over all $\MeSeeds$ seeds and the whole second half of every run
distorts $F$ by $\MeFsWorstDistort\,k_BT$ and leaves a minimum occupancy ratio of
$\MeFsWorstRatio$ against the $0.5$ deficit threshold. The classification is unchanged under the
most adverse reading of the data.

\subsection{What this system contributes}

Methane fails to need mFR on the \emph{state}, not on the \emph{coordinate} and not on the
\emph{baseline}. The collective variable resolves the solvent structure, the conditional mean
force is trustworthy, discovery is immediate and establishment is immediate. Every gate passes
and marginal reallocation is still unnecessary, because there is nothing in the landscape for it
to repair.

That is a different and cleaner boundary than the campaign's earlier negatives. Deca-alanine
fails because the conditional ensemble never equilibrates; the $R_{15}$ distance CV fails because
the state is never found; pentane's $\varphi_1$ fails because the coordinate cannot see the
states. Methane fails because the physics is easy --- which is the case a method paper most needs
and most rarely reports, since a system on which nothing happens produces no figure.

Its second contribution is calibration for the regime map. A $\MeBarrierHeight\,k_BT$ barrier
with a $\MeRefSpan\,k_BT$ total span is what \emph{ABF-sufficient} looks like in a real solvated
system, measured rather than asserted, and any future claim that a molecular benchmark is
establishment-limited can be read against it.

Following the preregistration, no mFR arm was run, no rate calibration was performed, and the
$\MeRunPs$~ps budget was \textbf{not} extended in search of a deficit. Had we raised it until one
appeared, the result would have been a statement about the budget rather than about methane.
