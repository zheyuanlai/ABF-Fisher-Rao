\section{The baseline itself can be wrong: deca-alanine}
\label{sec:deca}

Methane (\cref{sec:methane}) and sodium chloride (\cref{sec:nacl}) are negatives of the same
kind: every gate passes and marginal reallocation is unnecessary because there is no deficit to
repair. Deca-alanine is a negative of a different and more instructive kind. Its states are
plainly visible through the collective variable, its screen reports a population deficit, and an
early version of this study accordingly recorded that a Fisher--Rao arm was licensed. That
verdict is withheld, because the quantity ABF was estimating is wrong before any question about
reallocation can be asked.

This is the case that motivated Gate 0 and put it first in the frozen gate order, and it is
therefore load-bearing for every classification in \cref{sec:validity} rather than merely being
one more molecular system.

\subsection{Model and reference}

The system is Ace-(Ala)$_{10}$-Nme, $\DcAtoms$ atoms, in vacuum with $\DcForceField$, integrated
by BAOAB at $\DcTemp$~K with a $\DcDt$~fs timestep; the engine agrees with OpenMM to
$\DcParityE$ in energy and $\DcParityF$ in force. The collective variable is the distance between
the terminal carbonyl carbons, evaluated on $[\DcRlo, \DcRhi]$~nm with $\DcGridPts$ grid points
and soft walls at $\DcWallLo$ and $\DcWallHi$~nm.

One deviation is declared rather than discovered, and it is not small: the deca-alanine
literature uses CHARMM under NAMD, whereas $\DcForceField$ carries no CMAP correction. This is
the same molecule, the same coordinate and the same budget under a different modern force field,
and it is explicitly \emph{not} presented as a reproduction of any published profile.

The reference is umbrella sampling with MBAR over $\DcRefWindows$ windows at
$k = \DcRefKumb$~kJ/mol/nm$^2$, $\DcRefReplicas$ replicas per window, in $\DcRefBuilds$
interleaved builds each seeded from a different conformational pool, $\DcRefNsPerRep$~ns per
replica for $\DcRefAggNs$~ns aggregate --- roughly $144\times$ the literature ABF benchmark
budget. Builds agree to $\DcRefLtwo$~kJ/mol against a $\DcRefSpanKJ$~kJ/mol span, a ratio of
$\DcRefRatio$ against the $0.5$ acceptance threshold, in $\DcRefHours$~GPU-hours. The
time drift between checkpoints is $\DcRefDrift$~kJ/mol, or $\DcRefDriftFrac$ of a resolvable
effect and about five times the between-build spread, which is the honest limiting uncertainty
rather than the build agreement. The profile is single-basin and monotone with its minimum at
$\DcRefArgmin$~nm.

\subsection{Gate A passes, and that is the point}

The states are visible through the coordinate: the maximum pairwise total variation is
$\DcGateATV$ against a $0.30$ threshold, with $\DcGateALabels$ labels occupied. Deca-alanine is
the first system in this campaign where Gate A passes and ABF nevertheless fails, and the alkanes
and dipeptides never produced that combination. A study that stopped at Gate A --- as the
preregistration originally allowed --- would have read this as a licence.

\subsection{Gate 0 fails: the conditional ensemble never equilibrates}

The screen runs ABF only, $\DcScreenEnsembles$ ensembles of $\DcScreenWalkers$ walkers for
$\DcScreenNs$~ns each, $\DcScreenAggNs$~ns aggregate, which is the literature benchmark budget.
Three things go wrong together. The learned free energy spans
$\DcSpanLo$--$\DcSpanHi\,k_BT$ against the reference's $\DcRefSpanKT\,k_BT$. Between
$\DcPinLo$ and $\DcPinHi$ of walkers sit above $2.80$~nm through the whole second half, against
Gate 0's pinning clause of $0.90$; in one seed the population moves from entirely folded at
$0$~ps to entirely extended by $100$~ps and never returns. And the estimated mean force disagrees
with the reference derivative by $\DcGateZero$ of $\overline{|F'|}$, an error that persists at up
to $\DcGateZeroCounts$ effective counts per bin --- that is, it does not decay with sampling,
which is what distinguishes a wrong conditional ensemble from an undersampled one.

The attribution is not inferred, it is controlled. Running the \emph{same} local mean-force
estimator inside umbrella windows, where the conditional ensemble is held near equilibrium by the
restraint, gives a relative error of $\DcCtrlErr$; interior bins agree to better than one per
cent ($\DcCtrlBinA$ against $\DcCtrlBinAref$, $\DcCtrlBinB$ against $\DcCtrlBinBref$,
$\DcCtrlBinC$ against $\DcCtrlBinCref$), and integrating the windowed mean force yields
$\DcCtrlIntegrated\,k_BT$ against the reference's $\DcCtrlReference\,k_BT$ over the same range.
The estimator, the coordinate geometry, the reference and the integration are mutually
consistent. What fails is ABF's own conditional sampling.

\begin{remark}[Why this forecloses marginal reallocation]
\label{rem:deca_forecloses}
By \cref{prop:sel_invariance}, Fisher--Rao birth--death reallocates mass \emph{along} $\xi$ and
leaves $p_t(y \mid \xi)$ pointwise invariant. Deca-alanine's defect is precisely that
$p_t(y \mid \xi)$ is wrong: at a fixed end-to-end distance the backbone has not explored the
conformations that determine the mean force there. No amount of marginal reallocation can repair
a conditional it does not move, so the classification
\emph{conditional-equilibration-limited} is not merely a description of the failure but a
statement that this system cannot be an mFR candidate at any rate or budget. That is why Gate 0
is evaluated first and why failing it is a stop rather than a caution.
\end{remark}

Deliberately not done: the budget was not extended. $\DcScreenAggNs$~ns aggregate is the
literature benchmark run as the literature runs it, and raising it until the conditional
equilibrated would have produced a statement about the budget rather than about deca-alanine.

\subsection{A retracted screen, and two defects that both flattered the method}
\label{sec:deca_retraction}

An earlier screen at the same configuration hash reported \emph{establishment-limited} with a
Fisher--Rao arm licensed. It is retracted, and the two independent defects behind it are worth
stating because both made the result look more favourable, not less.

The first is edge clamping in the reference. Umbrella windows were bracketed slightly outside the
evaluation domain, and the binning routine clamped out-of-range samples into the edge bins rather
than dropping them. That piled $\DcRetClampFrac\,\%$ of all samples into bin zero and carved an
artificial $\DcRetFakeWell\,k_BT$ well at the domain edge, against neighbouring values around
$5.3\,k_BT$. The basin finder read that artifact as a genuine second minimum and split off a
$\DcRetFakeState$~nm ``state'' below the soft wall --- a region that can never hold a walker.
\emph{Gate C then fired on it.}

The second is that the minimum-count guard was declared and never applied. The mean-force routine
guarded only against a zero denominator, so a bin visited once contributed a single instantaneous
local mean force to the applied bias at full weight. The learned span inflated to
$\DcRetSpan\,k_BT$, a $42\,\%$ overestimate; $\DcRetPinned\,\%$ of walkers sat above $2.80$~nm in
the second half; and the folded basin's occupancy fell to $\DcRetOccupancy$. Since the bias-aware
target $Q^*$ is computed from that bias, Gate C was comparing a wrong occupancy against a wrong
target.

Both are fixed --- a per-bin trust ramp on the \emph{applied} bias, and dropping rather than
clamping out-of-domain samples, of which $\DcRefDropped$ were dropped in the accepted reference
--- and the fix carries a regression test that asserts the ramp actually reaches the applied
bias. We record that the declared field is NAMD's \texttt{fullSamples} and that the alanine
sampler in this same repository applied it correctly while the deca-alanine sampler did not, so
the defect was a divergence between two of our own implementations of one published convention
rather than a misreading of it.

Retracting that run did not rescue the verdict. The corrected screen fails Gate 0 for the
different and more fundamental reason above, and the surviving artifact still literally reads
\texttt{establishment-limited} with \texttt{licenses\_mfr: true}. That verdict is withheld in a
sibling file and must not be cited; we mention the discrepancy because a reader who greps the
results tree will find it.

\subsection{What this system contributes}

Deca-alanine supplies the campaign's only Gate 0 failure, and with it the calibration that makes
Gate 0 readable elsewhere: $\DcGateZero$ is what a broken conditional ensemble looks like on this
statistic, against $\GzWca$ for WCA, $\GzGateway$ for the gateway, $\NaGateZeroGlobal$ for NaCl
and $\MeGateZeroGlobal$ for methane. Without a measured failure the gate would be a threshold
nobody had ever seen fire.

It also supplies the campaign's cleanest demonstration that gate order is not cosmetic. Gate A
passes, the screen reports a deficit, and both facts are true and both are irrelevant, because
the object the deficit is measured against was estimated from an ensemble that had not
equilibrated. Read in the wrong order the same data licenses an mFR arm; read in the frozen order
it stops the study. The two retracted defects of \cref{sec:deca_retraction} are the same lesson
at the level of code rather than of protocol.

Finally, we note the asymmetry in how the two Gate A values are recorded: sibling artifacts in
the accepted reference report $\DcGateATV$ and $\DcGateATValt$ for the same quantity. Both clear
the threshold and the verdict is unaffected, but two values for one quantity is a defect even
when both are correct, and we quote the summary file.
