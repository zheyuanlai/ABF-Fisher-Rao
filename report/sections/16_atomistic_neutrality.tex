\section{Atomistic neutrality controls: two dipeptides}
\label{sec:neutrality}

The united-atom alkanes of \cref{sec:case_butane,sec:case_pentane} are deliberately simple,
and the limitation this study carried longest was that its molecular negative rested on a
single system with a single kind of coordinate. This section adds two all-atom peptides.
Both were selected in advance as candidates where mFR ought to help, both cleared every
screening gate that precedes the decisive one, and both then failed it in the same way:
\emph{ABF establishes the populations on its own}. They are reported because a screen that
only reports the systems that passed is not a screen.

The two are not redundant. Alanine dipeptide is the standard benchmark and was chosen for
familiarity; valine dipeptide was chosen for a specific reason --- a side-chain rotamer
barrier that ought to have produced a persistently under-populated state --- and it is the
stronger negative precisely because that reason turned out to be measurable, and wrong.

\subsection{Screening gates, and the one that decides}
\label{sec:neutrality_gates}
Both peptides were run through the same pre-registered sequence. A state map locates the
metastable states in the full torsional space. A \emph{distinguishability} gate then asks
whether the selected two-dimensional CV can resolve those states at all: marginal mFR sees
exactly $p(\xi)$, so if two states project onto overlapping regions of the CV, a deficit in
one is not a resolvable feature of $p(\xi)$ and no marginal reallocation could preferentially
clone into it. A coarse umbrella/MBAR reference with the omitted coordinate left \emph{free}
supplies the state populations. Only then does the decisive gate run.

That gate asks for a state that is \textbf{discovered early and stays under-populated}. It
requires, for each state $k$, the persistence-based hitting time $T_{\mathrm{hit},k}$ and
the establishment time $T_{\mathrm{est},k}$ measured against the \emph{bias-aware} target
\begin{equation}
  Q^\star_k(t) \;=\; \frac{\int_{C_k} e^{-\beta(F_{\mathrm{ref}}(z)-B_t(z))}\dd z}
                          {\int e^{-\beta(F_{\mathrm{ref}}(z)-B_t(z))}\dd z},
  \label{eq:biasaware}
\end{equation}
normalised over the reference's support with the observed populations conditioned the same
way. Scoring against the \emph{unbiased} populations instead would report a state as starved
exactly when ABF has correctly flattened it --- manufacturing the very signal mFR is meant to
remove, from a run in which nothing is wrong. Three outcomes are possible and only one
licenses going further: an important state not found early is \emph{discovery-limited}, the
$R_{15}$ regime where mFR cannot act; every state found \emph{and} established means
\emph{ABF is already sufficient}; a discovered but persistently under-populated state is the
regime mFR addresses. Both peptides returned the middle verdict.

\subsection{Alanine dipeptide: the mechanism ran, and changed nothing}
\label{sec:ala}
Vacuum Ace-Ala-Nme (22 atoms, AMBER ff14SB, 300~K, BAOAB at $\mathrm{d}t=1$~fs with
$\gamma=1$~ps$^{-1}$, no constraints and no hydrogen-mass repartitioning) on the standard
CV $\xi=(\varphi,\psi)$. The reference is umbrella sampling with MBAR over
$\AlaWindows$ windows and 16 independently thermalised copies each, $\AlaRefAgg~\mu$s
aggregate; a copy-level block bootstrap with MBAR re-solved on every replicate gives
$\Delta G(\varphi>0) = \AlaRefdG \pm \AlaRefdGse\,k_BT$ and a median FES standard error of
$\AlaRefFse\,k_BT$, with a further $\approx\AlaRefSyst\,k_BT$ inter-sampler systematic
carried explicitly from an independent OPES cross-check.

The reference settles the question before the sampler runs. The C7eq$\leftrightarrow$C7ax
barrier is a genuine $\AlaRareBarrier\,k_BT$, so the system is metastable and not trivially
easy --- but the internal barrier of $F(\psi\mid\varphi)$ is at most
$\AlaPsiBarrierMax\,k_BT$ at every populated $\varphi$ (median $\AlaPsiBarrierMed$). There is
no hidden slow coordinate: two-dimensional ABF on $(\varphi,\psi)$ biases the only slow
directions that exist.

The oracle pilot then confirms the consequence with the mechanism verifiably running.
Starting every walker in C7eq, plain ABF discovers C7ax at $\AlaRareHitLo$--$\AlaRareHitHi$~ps
in every seed and holds it at $\AlaRareOcc\%$ occupancy over the $\AlaWindow$~ps analysis
window --- against a reference equilibrium population of $\AlaRarePop$. The rare basin is not
merely reached; the transient ensemble \emph{over}-populates it. Against that baseline,
oracle mFR changes the kernel-matched integrated FES error by $\AlaGainMain\%$
$[\AlaGainCIlo, \AlaGainCIhi]$ on $\AlaGainWins$ paired seeds, against a preregistered
improvement bar of $\AlaThreshold\%$ --- a difference three orders of magnitude smaller than
the threshold, and roughly four orders below the reference's own uncertainty. Repeating from
a reference-equilibrium initialisation instead of a pure C7eq start gives $\AlaGainRefeq\%$.
Verdict: \textbf{\AlaVerdict}.

An FR-rate ladder spanning $\AlaEventSpan\times$ in realised replacement events
($\AlaEventLo \to \AlaEventHi$ over rates $\AlaRateLo \to \AlaRateHi$; the ratio of the
\emph{rates} is $22.5\times$ and of the per-opportunity intensities $20.4\times$, so the
denominator is worth naming) leaves the result flat: $\AlaGainMain\%$, $\AlaGainRateMid\%$,
$\AlaGainRateHi\%$. Over that same span the age-aware ancestor ESS falls from $\AlaEssHi$ to
$\AlaEssLo$ --- still above the $\AlaEssGate\,N$ floor, with $w_{\max} \le \AlaWmaxHi$. So the
null is not inertia: reallocation is demonstrably doing work to the ensemble, the cost of
that work is visible in the genealogy, and none of it reaches the free energy.

\begin{remark}[Two bugs that would each have produced this same verdict for the wrong reason]
\label{rem:ala_bugs}
Both were caught, and both are worth recording because each would have printed the word
\emph{equivalent} while the study measured nothing.

First, the accepted reference stores $+\infty$ in the 226 of 9409 cells no umbrella window
ever visited. Centring it computes $\infty - \infty$, so the oracle target is \texttt{NaN}
over the \emph{entire} grid, no birth or death weight is ever positive, and the oracle arm
silently degenerates into plain ABF --- reporting zero events and perfect equivalence with
itself. It was caught only because a test asserted that birth--death actually fires.

Second, the basin partition originally ranked reference minima \emph{by depth} and kept the
deepest few. That returns six shallow sub-minima inside the $\varphi<0$ megabasin and
\emph{omits C7ax entirely}, which is seventh by depth and is the one basin the study is
about. Prominence merging replaces it.

A third, in the reference analysis, is the most instructive: the ``internal barrier'' of
$F(\psi\mid\varphi)$ was computed as the column's global maximum, i.e.\ the height of the
sterically forbidden region rather than of any barrier a walker crosses. It reported a median
of $15.74\,k_BT$ and the conclusion ``$\psi$ is a hidden slow coordinate'' --- the exact
reverse of the truth, and the premise on which the entire study design rested. The corrected
periodic min--max barrier gives the $\AlaPsiBarrierMed\,k_BT$ quoted above. The lesson these
share is that a plausible summary statistic computed on the wrong domain fails silently and
in the direction that confirms the design.
\end{remark}

\subsection{Valine dipeptide: selected for a barrier that was not there}
\label{sec:val}
Ace--Val--Nme has genuine coupled backbone--rotamer structure. A lattice-seeded state map
over the whole $(\varphi,\psi,\chi_1)$ torus finds seven metastable states which factor
almost exactly as backbone megabasin $\times$ $\chi_1$ rotamer, and the selected CV
$\xi = (\varphi,\chi_1)$ recovers the full three-dimensional state label with $0.973$
cross-validated balanced accuracy and a worst pairwise footprint overlap of $0.189$, so a
population deficit would be visible to a marginal score. The umbrella reference on
$(\varphi,\chi_1)$, with $\psi$ free and started from four values, resolves $\ValRegions$
regions whose populations span $0.42$ down to $\ValRarePop$.

The decisive gate then fails outright. Over a $\ValTrun$~ps run with $\ValSeeds$ seeds of
$\ValWalkers$ walkers, ABF reaches every one of the $\ValRegions$ regions within
$\ValFirstTouch$~ps, holds them persistently from $\ValThit$~ps, and establishes every one
inside the $\pm50\%$ band by $\ValTest$~ps --- against a discovery threshold of $10\%$ of the
run. The worst relative deficit over the second half is $\ValDmax$ against a $0.50$
threshold, and no region sits below half its target for more than $\ValBelowHalf$ of the run
against a $\ValBelowHalfThresh$ threshold. The rarest region, at an unbiased population of
$\ValRarePop$, is nonetheless found by every seed and ends at $\ValRareOccTarget$ of its
bias-aware target. ABF's own free energy lands $\ValPmfErr\,k_BT$ from the reference
(seeds $\ValPmfErrLo$--$\ValPmfErrHi$; marginal TV $\ValMarginalTV$), so this is not a badly
converged run flattering itself. Verdict: \textbf{\ValVerdict}.

\paragraph{The reason valine was chosen was measurable, and wrong.} The system was selected
because a preliminary umbrella scan put the $\chi_1$ rotamer barrier at $11$--$18\,k_BT$,
which would make the rotamers effectively uncrossable and, on that reading, would have made a
$\chi_1$-hidden design discovery-limited. Direct measurement contradicts it: $\chi_1$
rotamers interconvert at $2.70$ changes per walker per ns, a rate that is flat from $30$ to
$300$~ps and identical for walkers seeded in a well and on a barrier --- an equilibrium rate,
not a seeding transient. The $11$--$18\,k_BT$ figures are \emph{backbone-clamped conditional}
barriers, measured with $\varphi$ and $\psi$ restrained at
$\kappa = 500$~kJ\,mol$^{-1}$rad$^{-2}$; the clamp forbids the backbone relaxation that
accompanies the rotation. With the backbone free the effective two-dimensional min--max path
costs $1.1$--$7.4\,k_BT$. The genuinely slow coordinate is $\varphi$, which crossed its
megabasin four times in $2581$~ns of aggregate unbiased sampling --- and $\varphi$ is in the
CV. That is the whole explanation for the verdict, and it is the same explanation as for
every other ABF-sufficient system in this study.

\begin{remark}[Conditional barriers are not the barrier a walker pays]
\label{rem:clamped}
Conditioning on the other coordinates can inflate a barrier severalfold: for valine the
$\chi_1$ barrier at fixed $\varphi$ has median $4.1\,k_BT$ and reaches $15.3$, while the
effective path with a free backbone costs $1.1$--$7.4$. A restrained scan is the natural way
to measure a torsional barrier and it systematically over-states the one that matters for
sampling. This is worth stating separately because it is the kind of error that survives
review: the measurement is correct, it is simply not a measurement of the quantity the design
depends on.
\end{remark}

\subsection{Three diagnostics that were wrong, and are worth not repeating}
\label{sec:neutrality_diagnostics}
The valine screen produced three measurement errors. None can change its verdict --- the
decisive condition fails by a wide margin, and two of the three touch conditions that only
ever gated a \emph{pass} --- but all three are properties of machinery meant to be reused,
and each is a mistake that produces a confident wrong answer rather than an obvious one.

\paragraph{An unpaired comparison in paired clothing.} The omitted-coordinate check compared
$p_{\mathrm{ABF}}(\psi\mid \text{region})$ against $p_{\mathrm{ref}}(\psi\mid \text{region})$
and reported a worst-region total variation of $\ValPsiOriginal$, apparently failing a
$\ValPsiThresh$ threshold. But ABF deliberately \emph{flattens} within a region while the
reference is Boltzmann-weighted inside it, so the two weight the region's interior
differently even when the $\psi$ conditional at every fixed $(\varphi,\chi_1)$ cell is
identical. Comparing cell by cell and aggregating with \emph{common} weights gives
$\ValPsiMatched$, and the condition passes. The signature was visible in the original numbers
--- the two largest, most internally varied regions disagreed while the six smaller ones did
not --- which is what a weighting artifact looks like and not what a genuine conditional
error looks like.

\paragraph{A counter that reads zero exactly when the barrier is real.} State entries were
counted only between \emph{consecutively labelled} frames. Reaching the $\varphi>0$ regions
means crossing a corridor above the region ceiling, where frames are unlabelled, so the
counter reported zero entries into $\ValEntriesNaive$ --- three regions with finite hitting
times that were demonstrably reached. The failure mode is perverse: the higher the barrier,
the more certainly the counter reads zero. Carrying the last labelled state across the
corridor gives $\ValEntriesCorridor$ entries respectively.

\paragraph{A target that concentrated where the reference knew nothing.} The first version of
\cref{eq:biasaware} capped cells the reference never filled at $F_{\min}+30\,k_BT$ and
normalised over the whole torus. Because ABF flattens, $B_t$ grows large exactly in those
cells and $e^{-\beta(F_{\mathrm{capped}}-B_t)}$ explodes there: $97\%$ of the target mass
landed in the cells the reference was least able to speak about. The fix is to define the
target on the reference's labelled support and condition the observed fractions the same way.
The diagnostic that caught it printed the offending number --- and was noticed only because a
human read the log. It is now an assertion, and the closure artifacts record
$\lvert\sum_k Q^\star_k - 1\rvert < 5\times10^{-16}$ and the matching statement for the
observed populations.

\subsection{What the two neutrality controls establish}
\input{tables/dipeptide_closure.tex}

\Cref{tab:dipeptide_closure} collects every dipeptide arm that was run. Two features of it
carry more weight than the numbers. First, the alanine arm is the \emph{oracle} target, built
from the reference free energy itself --- the quantity the method is trying to estimate --- so
what the table reports is the ideal-information control returning nothing, across a
$22\times$ range of realised event rate over which the age-aware ancestor ESS falls from
$\AlaEssHi$ to $\AlaEssLo$. The mechanism demonstrably worked harder and the accuracy did not
move. That is a stronger null than a practical arm would have produced, because a practical
arm's null is always partly attributable to a noisy online target. Second, valine has no row
at all: its establishment gate never fired, so no mFR arm was justified and none was run. Its
classification therefore rests on the ABF-only screen and not on a measured mFR outcome, and
we state that rather than presenting an absence as a null result.

Two independent all-atom systems, one of them selected specifically for a barrier that mFR
should have been able to exploit, both return the same verdict: when ABF's collective
variable contains the slow coordinate, ABF discovers \emph{and} establishes the populations
by itself, and marginal reallocation has no deficit to repair. This is not evidence that mFR
fails; it is evidence about which regime it belongs to, and it is what motivates constructing
that regime deliberately in \cref{sec:gateway} rather than continuing to sample molecules.

It also sets a standing prohibition that the screening plans state explicitly and that we
record here because the temptation is structural: both $T_{\mathrm{hit}} < 0.1\,T$ and
``starved for at least $0.2\,T$'' scale with run length, so shortening the run, cutting
walkers or widening the establishment band would manufacture a deficit rather than measure
one. The margins here are not close --- discovery is more than an order of magnitude faster
than required and the worst deficit is well under half its threshold --- and a negative
obtained at this margin is worth more than a positive obtained by retuning.
